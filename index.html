Countries in 5 Dimensions

Income → X, life expectancy → Y, schooling → Z, region → colour, population → size.

Quest controls: point with either controller. Pull the trigger on a country for details, or on a legend item to hide/show a region.

Desktop: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click points or legend items.

Data note: all figures are approximate, illustrative values assembled for this demo. They are not a citable statistical source.
